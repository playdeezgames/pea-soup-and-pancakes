Pea Soup and Pancakes has been playable on GitHub Pages since November 19. Today it gets a page here too.

It is chess crossed with Snake. You are a stack of pancakes, and you move like the last pea-soup piece you ate. Eat a knight and you hop. Eat a rook and you slide, with the rest of the stack trailing along behind on every square you landed on. The soup moves one piece a turn and bites where it can: a bite to the stack cuts it, a bite to the head ends lunch.

Eat the soup king to clear the bowl. It can't eat pancakes, so walk right up to it. The next bowl is thicker.

- Red squares are where the soup can bite next turn.
- Mouse, touch, keyboard and gamepad all work.
- Yer best score is kept in yer browser.

It's Thursday.

The latest version always lives at https://playdeezgames.github.io/pea-soup-and-pancakes/ and the itch build can lag behind it.
