## Page text

**Latest version:** https://playdeezgames.github.io/pea-soup-and-pancakes/

**Pea Soup and Pancakes** is chess crossed with Snake, at the Swedish Thursday lunch of pea soup followed by pancakes.

You are a stack of pancakes on a chessboard tablecloth. Your head moves like the last pea-soup piece you ate (like a king before your first meal), and your stack trails behind you on the squares you landed on. Every meal adds pancakes by its chess value: a pawn 1, a knight or bishop 3, a rook 5, a queen 9.

The soup fights back, one piece a turn. A piece that lands on your stack cuts it there. A piece that lands on your head ends lunch. Eat the soup king to clear the bowl. The next bowl is thicker.

- The soup king cannot eat pancakes. Corner it.
- Red squares are where the soup can bite next turn. Dots and gold boxes are where you can go.
- Pea-pawns spill from the pot and march down. Yer pawns march up, and both become queens on the far row.
- No legal move and yer stack goes cold.

It's Thursday.

### How to play

- **Mouse or touch:** tap a dotted square (or a gold-boxed pea) to move there.
- **Keyboard:** arrows or WASD move the cursor; Enter or Space moves there.
- **Gamepad:** d-pad or left stick, A or Start.

Yer best score is kept in yer browser.
